METHODOLOGY

How Proof Scores Work

Proof Scores are designed to evaluate the strength of the relationship between a digital metal asset and the physical metal it represents.

01
30 POINTS

Backing

Measures the strength and clarity of the physical backing claim.

  • Physical metal allocation
  • Quantity represented per token
  • Identification of underlying metal
  • Availability of supporting documentation
02
20 POINTS

Redemption

Measures the practical ability of holders to convert the digital claim into physical metal or its documented equivalent.

  • Redemption availability
  • Minimum redemption requirements
  • Geographic restrictions
  • Fees and procedures
03
20 POINTS

Custody

Measures the transparency and structure of physical metal custody.

  • Custodian identification
  • Storage arrangements
  • Segregation information
  • Audit or verification evidence
04
20 POINTS

Transparency

Measures how much independently verifiable information is available to the public.

  • Documentation
  • Reserve information
  • Attestations
  • Contract and issuer information
05
10 POINTS

Liquidity

Measures the observable market conditions surrounding the digital asset.

  • Trading volume
  • Market depth
  • Exchange availability
  • Bid/ask conditions

Reading the Score

IMPORTANT

Proof Scores are a proprietary research framework developed by Proof Of Metal. They are not credit ratings, investment recommendations, guarantees of physical reserves, or assessments of future performance.

Proof Of Metal exists to track the intersection of physical bullion and digital financial infrastructure.

Tokenized metal products are not interchangeable. Their backing, custody, redemption mechanisms, liquidity, and transparency can differ materially.

Proof Of Metal provides a common framework for comparing those characteristics alongside market price and physical metal benchmarks.
